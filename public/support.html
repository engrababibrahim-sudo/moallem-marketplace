Zoom Connection Help

  1. Sign in to your teacher account on Moallem.
  2. Choose the option to connect your Zoom account.
  3. Review and approve the permissions requested by Zoom.
  4. Return to Moallem and check the connection status.

If the connection fails, include the time of the attempt and a description of the error. Never send your password, client secret, or access tokens.
