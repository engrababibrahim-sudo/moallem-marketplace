Zoom Integration Guide

This guide explains how teachers use the Zoom integration with Moallem.

Connect an Account

Sign in to Moallem using a teacher account and select the Zoom connection option. Zoom will ask you to authorize the integration. Review the permissions before approving.

Lesson Meetings

After a successful connection, Moallem can use the authorized teacher account to create lesson meetings according to the permissions granted and the platform's available features.

Disconnect

You can revoke the application's access through your Zoom account. If you need assistance removing stored connection data from Moallem, contact platform support.

Troubleshooting

Confirm that you are signed in to the intended Zoom account. If authorization fails, record the error message and contact support.
