Moallem Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Moallem is an online tutoring platform that helps teachers and students arrange online lessons.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use the platform, we may process account information, profile details, lesson booking information, and information needed to connect a teacher's Zoom account.

Zoom Account Authorization

If a teacher connects Zoom, Moallem uses the permissions granted by that teacher to associate the teacher's Zoom account with the platform and create lesson meetings. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored for this purpose. We do not ask teachers to provide their Zoom passwords.

Use and Sharing

Information is used to operate the tutoring service, schedule lessons, provide support, and maintain platform security. Zoom information is sent to Zoom as necessary to provide the requested integration. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and Security

Information is retained only as needed for platform operations, legitimate business purposes, and applicable legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect stored information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. You can also revoke the Zoom integration through your Zoom account. Contact the platform administrator to exercise your rights or request disconnection and deletion of stored Zoom authorization tokens.
